# Weekly Review and Planning

Adapted from Hermes Agent (NousResearch, MIT licence; author Ben Barclay).

Default to recommendations and drafts. Change nothing without approval.

## 1. Set scope

Confirm timezone, review period, planning horizon, which task/CRM/calendar is the source of truth, and what you may change.

## 2. Calendar

Review the past week's meetings for implied follow-ups, then the next 1–2 weeks for deadlines, prep and capacity.

## 3. Capture inboxes

Go through task inbox, notes and flagged email. Classify each item: next action, project, waiting, scheduled, someday, reference, archive, or propose delete. State how many remain unprocessed.

## 4. Active projects / deals

For each: desired outcome, next action, owner, deadline, blocker, last activity, link. Flag anything with no next action, missed dates, duplicates or contradictory status.

## 5. Waiting and commitments

List promises the user made and items others owe. Propose follow-ups with dates and channel. Silence is not completion.

## 6. Capacity-aware plan

Estimate fixed calendar load, pick a small set of weekly outcomes plus next actions, ranked by consequence, deadline, dependency and effort. Do not fill every free hour. Name what is deferred.

## 7. Apply approved updates

Make only approved changes and read each back to confirm.

## Output

1. Wins and completed commitments
2. Overdue or at risk
3. Waiting / follow-ups
4. Stalled or unclear projects
5. Next week's outcomes and calendar constraints
6. Proposed updates awaiting approval
7. Gaps in coverage

## Pitfalls

Planning without checking calendar capacity; carrying every unfinished item forward as high priority; silently rescheduling or deleting commitments.
